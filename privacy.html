PRIVACY & PERSONALIZATION

How ZER0GAME handles your profile

The new dashboard uses a browser-local profile for now. Email registration and account syncing are not active yet. You can browse the library without creating an account.

Information saved on this device

Nickname, avatar image, favourite game identifiers, searches, game-page views and clicks on external Game, DLC or Cheat links are stored in your browser using local storage. Settings and the link queue are also stored in your browser. This information does not automatically synchronize between devices. Clearing browser storage will remove these locally saved details.

Control your activity

Open My Profile → Clear My Activity to remove recorded searches and link clicks. The profile reset control clears its saved name, avatar, favourites and activity. Saved queue links and dashboard settings can be cleared separately from their respective dialogs.

External services

Catalog cover images, fonts and download destinations may involve third-party services. Existing public download-click counters are maintained by a separate count API. Opening an external host does not provide ZER0GAME with a real transfer progress percentage or confirmation that any file finished downloading.

Future accounts

A separate secure OTP email registration system is planned for later. It will require a privacy and security review before activation. Public users will never receive permission to manage GitHub repository content merely by registering an account.
