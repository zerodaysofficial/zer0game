ACCOUNT & PRIVACY

How your account data works

ZER0GAME is a public catalog. No account is required to browse games. Registration is optional for your personal dashboard, favourites, avatar and activity history.

What we keep

When you sign in by email code, the authentication service processes your email and session. Your profile may contain a nickname and uploaded avatar. While signed in, ZER0GAME saves your search terms, pages viewed and clicks on Game, DLC and Cheat links. A download click is not proof that an external file was downloaded.

Who can see it

Your personal profile record, favourites and activity are restricted to your own authenticated account by database row-level security. Your avatar picture is publicly visible when displayed. Download-click counts shown on the catalog are aggregate numbers, not personally identifying activity.

Delete your data

You can delete your recorded activity at any time through My Profile → Clear My Activity. You can remove favourites by tapping the heart icon. To request full account deletion and the removal of avatar files, contact zer0daysofficial@proton.me. Avoid uploading sensitive images or using real names if you prefer to remain private.

Third-party services

The site may load cover images, fonts and external download links from third parties. Email sign-in and private data require a configured Supabase project. Your browser may retain a login session until you sign out. Cookies or storage needed for authentication are used only when you register and log in.
